## The ways of working with AI
I think there are healthy ways and sick ways to work with AI.  Now it is possible to adopt two ways for working with AI, the "traditional" one where you work with AI as peer with a Chat, the one that is mainly used and know. If you take time to figure out what they say and proceed by steps I do not see this that different than working with a peer exchaning informations and learning from the others. The typical way that AI will help you in this way is something like: I need to find a bound, the usual inequalities of your field does not work and you have no idea who/what could help/ask (also who would be willing to hear about your problems), you ask AI and AI will find from who know where, it happened to me they could found inequalities from Analytical Number Theory to give the bound, which of I course I would not have found. Also in my experience it also very is to get from the AI the reference to cite, so I do not agree that using AI you will not cite who deserve (unless you do want do it). On the contrary, with the enciclopedic knowledge your works will have much more probability to survive in time and be used when you do not expect in some distant future.
This mode is alos great to understand some literature(like reading a paper or a book) that is not in your background but you would like to enter into it to merge it with your research and estabilsh new brigdes. 

I do not see this modality of working a treat for Mathematics
