I start with a short personal history to tell you how I formed my opinions. 

At the end of 2022, for a mixed of reasons, I was tyring to understand which escape routes I had to leave my postdoc. I had read that in Europe they were making a Large Consortium called EuroHPC to give a GPU-based computing Infrastructure and they were going to hire many future unemployed Phds. I had a look at this GPus and I got that they were extraordinary. Little after GPT-3 came our for the average user and on my phone it appeared an application to work in the italian EuroHPC center. So I applied, I got some three interview and a job offer and accepted. After an initial period of "who knows what to do here" I was involved in project about training AI models for climate and weather and data anonynimization. 
But the problem was starting to work for real, given that I was neither able to submit "job" to run a code on this I turned to massive GPT-3 use to give some contributions to the projects. It was how frustrating since I running things and not understanding-knowing anything about it. But diving my task in enough small pieces and delegating them to GPT3  I could accomplish advanced engineering task. Many people around me were not realizing that I was more a prompter than an engineer, since I was doing what they were doing. At that time GPT was not the astonishing entity that is now, so most of people did not take it seriously and not using it, in particular if they were feeling expert of what they were doing.  I telling this because I always the impression, since the beginning that it was something big and not a "parraot"(as ironically said at the time by an expert of Stochastic Modelling) since with Google I could not have done anything close, my feeling it was that it could adapt to my requests.

Later at the beginning of 2025 I came back to live in Portugal for a job mainly for  teaching and in the meanwhile in the middle of 2026 I had the opportunity to have  new job in Mathematical Research. So I came back in Mathematics Research at a similar point where I was in 2023 with my new job. So I did not feel that things were dramatically changing in my daily job, given that I was restarting my old job without settled network, it was natural to recur to AI to look for help.
